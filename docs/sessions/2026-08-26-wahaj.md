## Second sitting: scale resolved, FTW-26 measured

### FTW-31, unitScale from the marker (Done)

An earlier note on FTW-14 claimed the capture had no marker. **That was wrong** — it was inferred
from the missing `metadata.json` rather than from the footage. Inspecting extracted frames 1 and
152 shows the marker flat on the table with the handwritten 170/170 and the date, visible across
the sequence. Correction recorded on the ticket. No retrain was needed: `unitScale` is a
measurement taken from the finished scene, not something training bakes in.

- First attempt was a dead end worth recording. Tried to find the marker in the **splat cloud** by
  colour and planarity heuristics: RANSAC found the tabletop plane, and a bright-neutral cluster
  search returned only 97 splats. Abandoned it rather than publish a number, because (a) 97 splats
  cannot locate a corner reliably, (b) the heuristic could not be verified to have found the marker
  rather than the phone or a floor highlight, and (c) it would have measured the **A4 sheet**
  (210x297 mm) rather than the **black border** (170x170 mm) — a silent ~20 percent error.
- `ns-viewer` was launched to pick corners by hand, but this viser build exposes no coordinate
  readout, so that route was dropped too.
- What worked: `tools/compute_unitscale.py`, detecting the border in **image space** and
  triangulating through the COLMAP poses in `transforms.json`. Marker found in **102 of 304 frames**,
  **27 of 28** view pairs self-consistent, **unitScale 0.369573** (1 unit = 0.3696 m), spread
  **2.1 percent**. Verified against a debug overlay: the detected quad sits on the outer edge of the
  black border, corners correctly ordered, ignoring paper, table and glasses.
- Independent plausibility check: at this scale the bounding box is **9.3 x 7.0 x 8.8 m**, a sane
  room. Recovering 170.0 mm from the marker that defined the scale is circular and is recorded as
  such; the 2.1 percent spread is the honest uncertainty.
- The tool refuses rather than guesses: four edges must agree and both diagonals sit at sqrt(2) x
  edge within tolerance, else it exits emitting nothing.
